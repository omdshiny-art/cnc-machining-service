Technical Whitepaper: Precision CNC Machining Service

This document outlines the manufacturing capabilities, tolerance benchmarks, and quality control protocols for high-end industrial components.

I. Engineering Scope & Dimensional Control

A professional CNC machining service is evaluated by its ability to maintain consistent Cpk levels across complex geometries. We integrate high-speed spindle technology with advanced toolpath optimization to achieve sub-micron precision.

Multi-Axis Milling

Utilizing 5-axis linkage for complex aerospace and medical components. Standard linear tolerance: ±0.005mm. Surface finish capabilities up to Ra 0.4μm.

Ref: Advanced Milling Specifications

Complex Turning

Swiss-type and multi-turret lathe operations for intricate shaft components. Specializing in difficult-to-cut materials including Titanium Gr5 and 17-4 PH Stainless Steel.
