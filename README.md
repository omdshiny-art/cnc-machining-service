# CNC Machining Services & Precision Component Manufacturing

This repository serves as the technical documentation and project overview for our **CNC machining services**, specializing in high-precision component manufacturing for industrial and retail sectors. We focus on delivering components with tight tolerances and superior surface finishes.

## 🛠 Technical Capabilities (Technical SEO Section)

We provide specialized machining solutions for complex geometries and demanding material requirements.

* **Precision Standards:** Consistently achieving tolerances within **±0.005mm** (or **±0.001mm** upon specific technical review).
* **Core Services:** * **CNC Milling:** 3-axis, 4-axis, and 5-axis simultaneous machining.
    * **CNC Turning:** High-speed precision lathes for complex cylindrical components.
    * **Surface Treatment:** Anodizing, powder coating, bead blasting, and passivation.
* **Material Expertise:** * **Metals:** Aluminum (6061, 7075), Stainless Steel (304, 316), Titanium (Gr2, Gr5), Brass, and Copper.
    * **Plastics:** PEEK, POM (Delrin), Nylon, and PTFE.

## 📐 Industry Applications

Our production processes are optimized for procurement engineers and project managers in the following fields:

1.  **Industrial Hardware:** Heavy-duty structural components and custom fasteners.
2.  **Home & Retail:** High-end architectural hardware, including precision-engineered wall hooks and bathroom fittings.
3.  **Automotive & Robotics:** Custom brackets, housings, and mechanical assemblies.

## 🔬 Quality Assurance & Inspection

Quality is managed through a rigorous "Master Craftsman" approach combined with modern metrology:

* **Pre-production:** Material certification (MTR) and DFM (Design for Manufacturing) feedback.
* **In-process:** Dimensional inspection at critical stages.
* **Final Inspection:** Full reporting using CMM, micrometers, and thread gauges to ensure zero-defect delivery.
